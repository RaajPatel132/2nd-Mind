Creamy carbonara without cream

A proper carbonara has no cream in it at all. The sauce comes from egg yolks, finely grated pecorino and a ladle of the starchy water the pasta cooked in, and the trick is to stir them together off the heat so the eggs turn glossy instead of scrambling.

Start by frying cubes of cured pork cheek slowly until the fat runs and the edges crisp. Boil the spaghetti in well salted water, and keep a mug of that water before you drain. Whisk four yolks with a generous handful of cheese and plenty of black pepper in a large bowl.

Toss the hot pasta into the pork pan for half a minute, then tip everything into the bowl and stir quickly, adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce coats each strand. Serve at once with more pepper and cheese, because carbonara does not wait for anyone.
