Why the night bus routes were redrawn

The city's night bus network was redrawn this summer after a review found that most late riders were travelling between the hospital district and the two railway stations, not through the old centre where the routes used to run. The new lines cut across the river at the north bridge and skip the market square entirely.

Planners say the change adds eleven minutes to a trip for people who live near the market, but saves twenty minutes for the nurses and station staff who make up the largest group of night passengers. Service will run every thirty minutes until one in the morning and hourly after that.

Residents of the market quarter have asked for a single loop line to be kept as a compromise. The transport committee will vote on that request in November, and a trial is planned for the winter timetable if it passes.
