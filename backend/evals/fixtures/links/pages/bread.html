A slow loaf for lazy weekends

This loaf asks for almost nothing of your time. Mix four hundred grams of flour, three hundred of water, a spoon of salt and a pinch of yeast in the evening, cover the bowl, and leave it on the counter overnight. By morning the dough has more than doubled and is covered in bubbles.

Fold the dough over itself a few times on a floured surface, shape it into a round, and let it rest while the oven and a heavy lidded pot heat to the highest setting. Bake with the lid on for thirty minutes, then take it off and give the crust another fifteen minutes to darken.

The long rest is what gives the crumb its open texture and the crust its blistered, crackling surface, and it means the only work on the day you eat the bread is shaping it.
